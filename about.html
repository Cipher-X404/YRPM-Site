The defining milestones that shaped Yashua Resurrection Power Ministry.

2000
Genesis Mandate

The Ministry Inception

In the year 2000, under the divine guidance of the Holy Spirit, the ministry was birthed. Headed by Apostle Polycarp Ukwudiri Samuel alongside Evangelist Polycarp Comfort Amarachi, the assignment began as an earnest prayer altar and evangelistic outreach devoted to restoring broken lives and unveiling resurrection life.

Daily Intercession & Evangelistic Outreaches
2003
Ecclesia Ordained

Established as a Full Church

By 2003, the expanding community of believers transitioned from a fellowship ministry into a fully consecrated church assembly. Doors opened wide to welcome worshippers into pastoral discipleship, kingdom sacraments, and structured spiritual growth under apostolic oversight.

Formation of Choir, Ministries, and Auxiliaries
2015
Prophetic Revelation

The Sabbath Restoration

Though the church was formerly a Sunday-keeping congregation, in 2015 the Holy Spirit spoke unequivocally to the mantle holders through scripture: "Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy." In radical obedience and disregarding human customs, YRPM shifted completely to observing the biblical Seventh-Day Sabbath.

Restored Alignment with the Eternal Fourth Commandment
